THE STORY BEHIND SKY READY™

FROM
HIKE TO LAB

What started with a hike and a little Parrot drone slowly evolved into something much bigger.

2014

THE FIRST FLIGHT

We started Sky Ready™ back in 2014, when I was living abroad, surrounded by mountains and woods and spending a lot of time outdoors. I was a big "hiking aficionado," always looking for new ways to explore and document the places I was discovering. At some point, a simple idea came up: what if we could take a selfie from far away, or actually record everything around us while we were out exploring? That's when we did our research, found drones, and learned what they could do, and that's when we fell in love with them.

That curiosity led us to our first drone, a Parrot. We had no idea what we were doing. We didn't know photography, video, or filmmaking, and we definitely didn't know that flying a camera was going to turn into something much bigger. At the time, it was simply recreation, fun, and a way to record our hikes and the places we were exploring.

Then came our second drone, a Mavic Pro. That's when we realized something: we had a drone, but we had no idea what we were doing with photography or video.

We wanted to learn. An opportunity came up for me to study cinematography in Mexico City, so we moved to CDMX and enrolled at Facultad de Cine, in La Roma, to study film. We wanted to understand more than just how to fly. We wanted to learn how to create images, tell stories, work with light, movement, and composition, and ultimately understand how to use a camera as a storytelling tool.

2017

THE BIGGER VISION

We eventually stepped up to a high-end Inspire 2, got licensed and insured, and officially became SKYRDY™ — short for Sky Ready — Drone Video + Motion Lab. In 2017, we established SKYRDY™ in Mexico City and began our journey into the film and television industry.

For a few months, things started moving in the direction we had dreamed about. Then the earthquake hit. Film and television production came to a halt, and once again, life forced us to change course. I relocated to Baja, where I continued working with local talent, taking on smaller gigs, and finding ways to keep creating.

2020

WE KEEP LEARNING

Then, in 2020, the pandemic hit. This time, we had to stop for a while. But stopping didn't mean giving up. We kept learning. We completed a couple of online courses and turned to what we jokingly call "YouTube University," and continued teaching ourselves, experimenting, practicing, and figuring things out one project at a time.

Along the way, we grew the team, expanded our services, improved our equipment, refined our skills, and, most importantly, developed a much clearer vision of what SKYRDY™ could become.

TODAY

WE KEEP FLYING

What started with a hike and a little Parrot drone slowly evolved into something much bigger. Today, we are relaunching something that was once just a dream. After all these years, that dream is still alive and well. And we're still doing what we've always loved:

Keep learning. Keep growing. Keep experimenting. Keep filming. Keep flying.
